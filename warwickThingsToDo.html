Things To Do in Warwick!

Visit The Warwick Mall

The Warwick Mall is one of the main shopping destinations in the city. It features a mix of popular retail stores, restaurants, and a movie theater, making it a convenient place to shop, eat, or just spend an afternoon indoors. The mall also hosts community events throughout the year, adding to its lively atmosphere. Be careful with the theater's comfy reclining seats, it’s very easy to fall asleep on!

Visit Oakland Beach

Oakland Beach is one of Warwick’s most popular coastal spots. It’s a great place to relax by the water, enjoy a walk along the beach, or watch the sunset. The area often has a friendly, local feel, with people fishing, riding bikes, or simply taking in the ocean breeze.

Go to Local Seafood Restaurants!

Warwick is well known for its delicious seafood. Many local restaurants near the coast serve fresh fish, clams, and chowder, perfect for anyone craving a classic Rhode Island meal. Whether it is a casual cam shack or a sit down seafood spot, you'll always find something tasty nearby. A well known one and town favorite is Iggy's Doughboys & Chowder House.

Go to Lil Rhody's IceCream Shop!

Lil Rhody is one of my personal favorites. One thing they’re definitely not is stingy with their portions! A small at Lil Rhody is basically a large anywhere else. Despite the generous servings, their prices are still really reasonable and affordable.
