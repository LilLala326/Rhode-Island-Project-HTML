Things to do in Providence!

Attend A Waterfire Event!

A famous seasonal art installation where bonfires are lit on all three rivers of downtown Providence, often accompanied by music and performances. It’s one of the most iconic experiences in Providence and brings people together for a unique celebration of light and art.

Visit The RISD Museum!

The museum connected to the Rhode Island School of Design featured an impressive collection of artwork from around the world, including paintings, sculptures, textiles, and decorative arts. It’s a great place to explore creativity and culture in the heart of the city.

Visit Federal Hill!

Known as Providence’s “Little Italy,” Federal Hill is full of authentic Italian restaurants, bakeries, and cafes. Walking down Atwells Avenue, you’ll find lively patios, cute twinkling lights, and some of the best food in the state.

Visit Thayer Street!

Located near Brown University, Thayer Street is a popular hangout spot with its variety of restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, and street energy. It’s a fun place to spend time with friends or just explore something new.

Visit Roger Williams Park!

Roger Williams Park, named after the founder of the city of Providence Roger Williams, has been a favorite destination for both Providence and Rhode Island residents. Major attractions include the nationally recognized Roger Williams Zoo, the Botanical Center, Carousel Village, and the Temple to Music.
