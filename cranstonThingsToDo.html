Things To Do In Cranston!

Visit Garden City!

Garden City is one of Cranston’s most popular destinations. It’s an open-air shopping center with a mix of well-known stores, local shops, and restaurants. During the warmer months, it’s also a great place to stroll around, grab a coffee, and enjoy outdoor concerts and seasonal events. During the summer they even transform their gazebo into an open air Farmers Market on every Sunday!

Visit Rolfe Square!

Rolfe Square is filled with local charm and small businesses that give Cranston its friendly personality. From cozy cafes and restaurants to specialty stores, this area captures the community spirit that makes Cranston feel like home. My favorite breakfast spot, Mesa Cafe and Grill, is on this square!!

Visit Pawtuxet Village!

Pawtuxet Village is a charming historic neighborhood shared between Cranston and Warwick, known for its beautiful scenery, walkable streets, and waterfront views. It’s the perfect place to take a relaxing stroll, enjoy the cute local shops, and grab a bite to eat. My favorite spots here are Crepe Corner and Dear Hearts Ice Cream! I grew up getting ice cream from here with my Mother!
