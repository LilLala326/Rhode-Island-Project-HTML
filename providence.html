Providence

Providence, the capital and most populated city in Rhode Island, is known for its amazing culinary scene, vibrant arts and culture, and the restoration of its rivers – especially the Waterfire art installation that lights up the city’s waterways.

The city is heaven for food lovers, offering a diverse mix of restaurants and unique dining experiences. Popular spots like Federal Hill, famous for its Italian cuisine, and Thayer Street, known for its variety of cafes and local eats, make Providence a perfect place for anyone who enjoys food and atmosphere.

Providence is also recognized for its institutions like the RISD Museum and the beautiful Roger Williams Park, a place I personally love visiting to take walks and enjoy nature!
