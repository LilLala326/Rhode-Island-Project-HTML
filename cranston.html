Cranston

Cranston is the second most populated city in Rhode island and the place I currently call home!

The city has many great spots to visit like Garden City, a popular outdoor shopping center filled with stores, restaurants, and events; Rolfe Square, known for its local businesses and community feel; The Historic Park Theatre, a beautifully restored venue that hosts concerts, shows, and community events; and Pawtuxet Village, a charming waterfront area known for its scenic views, cozy cafes, and great food spots. Together, these places add to Cranston's lively arts, dining, and community scene.
