Warwick

Warwick is the third most populated city in Rhode Island. It’s known for its mix of shopping, dining, and beautiful coastal spots that make it a great place to explore and relax.

One of the city’s most well known destinations is the Warwick Mall, which offers a variety of stores, restaurants, and entertainment options all in one place. For those who enjoy the water, Oakland Beach is a local favorite where people go to enjoy the view, take a walk along the shore, or grab a bite to eat at one of the nearby seafood restaurants. If you're ever craving ice scream, Lil Rhody is the place to go!

Warwick’s blend of convenience, community, and coastal charm makes it a great place to live and visit.
