About Rhode Island

Rhode Island may be the smallest state in the United States, but it holds a big place in history and culture. Despite its size, it’s known for its beautiful coastline, hence the nicknames "Little Rhody" and “The Ocean State”, its lively communities and its deep connection to the beginnings of America.

Rhode Island’s history began with Roger Williams founding Providence for religious freedom after being banished from Massachusetts. It was also the last of the original thirteen colonies to ratify the U.S Constitution. Today, Rhode Island’s three largest cities – Providence, Cranston, and Warwick – each offer their own character and charm.

From Providence’s creative energy and historic neighborhoods, to Cranston’s welcoming communities, and Warwick’s scenic coastal spots, these cities together reflect the heart of Rhode Island. I have lived in all three of these cities!!
